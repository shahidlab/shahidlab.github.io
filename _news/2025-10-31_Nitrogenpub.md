Our collaborative work on effect of experimental warming on the free-living N fixing microbial community in a tropical forest is now published in [New Phytologist](https://nph.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/nph.70592). This was led by the amazing Parker Bartz and Benedicte Bachelot (Oklahoma State University).
