## Life in Sheffield

Located in a vibrant and affordable student city, Sheffield offers the best of both worlds: a thriving academic community surrounded by green space, with the Peak District on the doorstep and excellent transport links to major UK cities. See [here](https://usic.sheffield.ac.uk/international-college/about-university-of-sheffield/living-in-sheffield) for more about life in Sheffield.
